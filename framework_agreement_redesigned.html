Framework Agreement

For the Purchase of Accounts Receivable

Docusign Envelope ID: 0CB1E72C-7FC5-4111-BCA5-78189067D3A0 Effective Date: 18 July 2024

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the following meanings:

Account Receivable
Means the right to receive any and all present and future payments of money due and payable, whether due now or payable in the future (including any entitlement to late payment interest whether by contract or implied by law) as a result of an Underlying Relationship.
Advance Rate
Means a percentage of the face value of the Account Receivable (as may be adjusted by MODIFI from time to time).
Adverse Claim
Means any Security Interest, netting, set-off, claim, defence or counterclaim, prohibition on assignment, negative pledge, including claims related to shipment, delivery, damage, defect, or performance.
Applicable Law
Means all existing and future laws, rules, regulations, statutes, treaties, codes, ordinances, Permits, certificates, orders and licenses of any governmental authority.
Business Day
Means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday, or public holiday in Amsterdam on which the banks in Amsterdam are open for general business.
Deferred Purchase Price
Means with respect to an Account Receivable the higher of (i) zero; and (ii) the amount actually Collected less the Initial Purchase Price, Fees, and any amounts payable by the Seller to MODIFI.
Event of Insolvency
Deemed to occur if a Person:
  • Stops or suspends payment of its debts.
  • Begins negotiations for rescheduling debts.
  • Files a petition for bankruptcy or winding-up.
  • Has a receiver or manager appointed over its assets.
Political Risk
Includes non-payment by a Foreign Public Buyer, war, civil war, currency shortages, or government decrees preventing payment.
1.2 Interpretation

1.2.1 Words and phrases defined in the Companies Act 2006 shall have an equivalent meaning.

1.2.6 Any phrase introduced by the words “including” or “in particular” shall be construed as illustrative only.

2. Offer

2.1 The Seller may provide information regarding Accounts Receivable via the Platform. MODIFI will display the applicable Advance Rate and Fees.
2.2 Posting an Invoice indicates acceptance of the Fees and purchase conditions.

3. Sale and Purchase of Accounts Receivable

3.1 MODIFI may, but shall not be obliged to, accept an offer made by the Seller in accordance with Clause 2 (Offer) by indicating its acceptance via the Platform.
3.2 On notification of acceptance by MODIFI, Seller shall have sold and assigned absolutely all of its present and future right, title and interest in, to and under the relevant Account Receivable and all Related Rights in relation thereto to MODIFI.
3.3 On purchase of an Account Receivable MODIFI may notify the relevant Buyer of such purchase either directly or via the Platform and provide account details for payment.
3.5 If a State of Default occurs prior to MODIFI making payment of the Initial Purchase Price, MODIFI shall be released from its obligation to pay the Initial Purchase Price and shall instead only pay the Deferred Purchase Price.

4. Payment of Purchase Price

4.2 MODIFI shall pay the Initial Purchase Price on the IPP Payment Date (within 3 Business Days of acceptance).
4.3 Deferred Purchase Price is paid within 2 Business Days of the Account being Collected.

11. Indemnities

11.1 Seller shall indemnify MODIFI against any losses arising from breach of agreement, tax liabilities, or disputes related to Accounts Receivable.
11.3 Late Fees of 1.5% per month apply if Collected after the Late Fee Start Date.

17. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

17.1 This Agreement shall be governed by English Law.
17.2 Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the English courts.
