1. Scope of Framework Agreement

1.1
Seller wishes to use trade finance solutions offered on the WHIZUNIK Platform, including receivables financing, invoice factoring, export financing, seller financing, and supply chain finance.
1.2
This Agreement governs receivables purchase transactions executed between WHIZUNIK and the Seller and sets out the commercial, operational, and legal terms that apply to each Invoice accepted for purchase.
1.3
Seller receives controlled access to platform-based financing solutions, subject to eligibility, compliance, and risk assessment requirements determined by WHIZUNIK.

3. Definitions and Interpretation

For the purposes of this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms shall have the meanings assigned to them below:

3.1 Account Receivable
“Account Receivable” means any present or future monetary obligation, payment entitlement, receivable, invoice amount, trade debt, or financial claim arising from the sale of goods or provision of services by the Seller to a Buyer under an Underlying Relationship, whether or not such amount is due, payable, earned, invoiced, matured, disputed, contingent, or subject to deferred payment terms.
3.2 Addendum
“Addendum” means any amendment, supplemental agreement, annexure, side letter, transaction confirmation, or written modification executed between WHIZUNIK and the Seller in connection with this Agreement.
3.3 Advance Rate
“Advance Rate” means the percentage of the approved Invoice value that WHIZUNIK agrees to disburse to the Seller as the Initial Purchase Price prior to Collection from the Buyer.
3.4 Adverse Claim
“Adverse Claim” means any lien, pledge, charge, mortgage, encumbrance, assignment, security interest, retention right, offset, counterclaim, attachment, garnishment, injunction, or third-party claim affecting ownership, transferability, enforceability, or collectability of an Account Receivable.
3.5 Agreement
“Agreement” means this Framework Agreement together with these Receivables Purchase Terms and Conditions (“RPTC”), all Schedules, Addenda, Transaction Confirmations, platform acknowledgments, amendments, and ancillary documents executed between the Parties.
3.6 Applicable Law
“Applicable Law” means all applicable statutes, laws, ordinances, regulations, rules, governmental orders, sanctions regimes, export control laws, anti-money laundering regulations, anti-bribery requirements, judicial decisions, and regulatory guidance applicable to either Party, the Platform, or any transaction contemplated herein.
3.7 Buyer
“Buyer” means the purchaser, importer, customer, debtor, obligor, or entity responsible for payment of an Invoice issued by the Seller.
3.8 Collection
“Collection” means any payment, settlement, recovery, transfer, remittance, proceeds, or realization received in respect of an Account Receivable, whether received directly or indirectly, in whole or in part.
3.9 Deferred Purchase Price
“Deferred Purchase Price” means the residual balance payable by WHIZUNIK to the Seller following Collection of the relevant Account Receivable after deduction of all Fees, adjustments, penalties, deductions, chargebacks, taxes, and other applicable amounts.
3.10 Due Date
“Due Date” means the date specified in the Invoice or Underlying Relationship on which payment by the Buyer becomes contractually due and payable.
3.11 Event of Insolvency
“Event of Insolvency” means any bankruptcy, insolvency, liquidation, administration, restructuring, winding-up, receivership, business rescue proceeding, moratorium, creditor arrangement, suspension of payments, or analogous event affecting the Seller or Buyer.
3.12 Factoring Fee
“Factoring Fee” means the financing and servicing fee charged by WHIZUNIK in connection with the purchase, management, administration, and collection of Accounts Receivable.
3.13 Fees
“Fees” means collectively the Transaction Fee, Factoring Fee, Processing Fee, Set-Up Fee, Late Fee, legal expenses, recovery expenses, administrative charges, and any other charges disclosed through the Platform or Transaction Confirmation.
3.14 Initial Purchase Price
“Initial Purchase Price” means the amount disbursed by WHIZUNIK to the Seller on the Purchase Date, calculated based on the applicable Advance Rate and net of Fees.
3.15 Intellectual Property
“Intellectual Property” means all patents, copyrights, trademarks, service marks, software rights, databases, trade secrets, proprietary systems, algorithms, branding, domain names, platform architecture, know-how, confidential processes, and related rights owned or licensed by WHIZUNIK.
3.16 Invoice
“Invoice” means a valid, genuine, enforceable, and undisputed commercial invoice issued by the Seller to a Buyer evidencing the sale of goods or provision of services under an Underlying Relationship.
3.17 Late Fee
“Late Fee” means a monthly compounding servicing fee or default fee charged on overdue amounts outstanding beyond the Due Date or beyond the permitted Payment Term.
3.18 Payment Term
“Payment Term” means the agreed duration between the Invoice date and the Due Date, including any approved deferred payment period.
3.19 Platform
“Platform” means the WHIZUNIK digital financing and receivables management platform through which transactions are initiated, reviewed, approved, monitored, serviced, and administered.
3.20 Political Risk
“Political Risk” means any governmental action, sovereign event, currency restriction, sanctions measure, trade embargo, war, civil unrest, expropriation, transfer restriction, import/export prohibition, or geopolitical circumstance affecting payment obligations or transfer of funds.
3.21 Portfolio
“Portfolio” means the aggregate pool of Accounts Receivable purchased, financed, managed, or serviced by WHIZUNIK for the Seller.
3.22 Processing Fee
“Processing Fee” means the operational, administrative, compliance, verification, onboarding, technology, documentation, or platform usage fee charged by WHIZUNIK.
3.23 Purchase Price
“Purchase Price” means the aggregate consideration payable by WHIZUNIK for an Account Receivable, consisting of the Initial Purchase Price and the Deferred Purchase Price.
3.24 Related Rights
“Related Rights” means all rights, remedies, guarantees, insurance proceeds, supporting security, collateral rights, collection rights, contractual remedies, and ancillary entitlements associated with an Account Receivable.
3.25 Repurchase Price
“Repurchase Price” means the total amount payable by the Seller to reacquire or repurchase an Account Receivable following a breach, dispute, fraud event, State of Default, or other triggering event under this Agreement.
3.26 Schedule
“Schedule” means any schedule, annexure, appendix, exhibit, or attachment forming part of this Agreement, including Schedule 1 and Schedule 2.
3.27 Security Interest
“Security Interest” means any mortgage, lien, pledge, assignment by way of security, retention arrangement, collateral right, hypothecation, charge, or other encumbrance.
3.28 Seller Account
“Seller Account” means the verified bank account maintained by the Seller and registered on the Platform for receiving payments under this Agreement.
3.29 Set-Up Fee
“Set-Up Fee” means the onboarding, account establishment, due diligence, compliance, or integration fee charged by WHIZUNIK at commencement of the relationship.
3.30 State of Default
“State of Default” means any breach, insolvency event, fraudulent activity, payment failure, dispute, misrepresentation, compliance violation, or other event entitling WHIZUNIK to suspend financing, demand repurchase, or terminate this Agreement.
3.31 Transaction Fee
“Transaction Fee” means the fee charged by WHIZUNIK for purchasing, processing, structuring, or administering each Account Receivable transaction.
3.32 Transfer
“Transfer” means the assignment, sale, conveyance, transfer, or disposition of legal and beneficial rights relating to an Account Receivable from the Seller to WHIZUNIK.
3.33 Underlying Relationship
“Underlying Relationship” means the contractual, commercial, purchase order, supply agreement, trade arrangement, or business relationship between the Seller and Buyer giving rise to an Invoice or Account Receivable.

4. Offer

4.1
The Seller may upload Invoices, shipping records, purchase orders, delivery confirmations, trade documents, and related supporting materials onto the Platform for the purpose of offering the related Accounts Receivable for purchase by WHIZUNIK.
4.2
Submission of an Invoice through the Platform constitutes a legally binding offer by the Seller to sell, assign, and transfer the related Account Receivable together with all Related Rights to WHIZUNIK in accordance with this Agreement.
4.3
WHIZUNIK may review, verify, evaluate, approve, conditionally approve, or reject any proposed transaction in its sole and absolute discretion, taking into account underwriting standards, risk policies, sanctions screening, compliance checks, portfolio concentration limits, and internal credit assessment criteria.
4.4
WHIZUNIK may request additional information, trade documentation, Buyer confirmations, customs records, shipping documentation, bank statements, or financial records prior to approving any transaction.
4.5
Upon provisional acceptance, the Platform shall display:
  • applicable Fees,
  • Advance Rate,
  • estimated Purchase Price,
  • payment timelines,
  • currency conversion information,
  • and any special conditions applicable to the transaction.
4.6
The Seller confirms and accepts all displayed terms by digitally approving or submitting the transaction through the Platform.
4.7
WHIZUNIK reserves the right to revoke or amend any provisional approval prior to disbursement if adverse information, fraud indicators, sanctions concerns, compliance risks, or material changes are identified.

5. Sale and Purchase of Accounts Receivable

5.1
Upon acceptance by WHIZUNIK, the Seller irrevocably sells, assigns, transfers, conveys, and grants to WHIZUNIK all legal and beneficial rights, title, and interest in and to the relevant Accounts Receivable together with all Related Rights.
5.2
The Parties acknowledge and agree that each Transfer is intended to constitute a true sale transaction and not merely the creation of security.
5.3
The Seller shall execute all notices, acknowledgments, assignments, and confirmations required to perfect or evidence the Transfer of the relevant Accounts Receivable.
5.4
WHIZUNIK may notify the Buyer of the Transfer and direct the Buyer to remit payment exclusively to bank accounts designated by WHIZUNIK.
5.5
The Seller shall not:
  • reassign,
  • pledge,
  • transfer,
  • discount,
  • securitize,
  • or otherwise encumber any transferred Account Receivable.
5.6
The Seller shall promptly notify WHIZUNIK upon becoming aware of:
  • Buyer disputes,
  • offsets,
  • counterclaims,
  • credit notes,
  • delayed shipments,
  • defective goods,
  • payment delays,
  • or any event affecting collectability.
5.7
Political Risk, currency restrictions, transfer restrictions, Buyer insolvency, sanctions events, or sovereign actions shall not relieve the Seller from obligations arising under this Agreement where a repurchase event has occurred.
5.8
WHIZUNIK may require invoice assignment notices, payment redirection notices, acknowledgment letters, or Buyer confirmations in such form as WHIZUNIK determines appropriate.
5.9
The Seller acknowledges that WHIZUNIK may rely upon electronic records, platform confirmations, and uploaded documentation as conclusive evidence of the transaction.
5.10
The Seller represents that all transferred Accounts Receivable:
  • are genuine,
  • arise from bona fide commercial transactions,
  • are free from Adverse Claims,
  • and are legally enforceable against the relevant Buyer.
