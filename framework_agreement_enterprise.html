1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the following meanings:

“Account Receivable”means the right to receive any and all present and future payments of money due and payable, whether due now or payable in the future (including any entitlement to late payment interest whether by contract or implied by law) as a result of an Underlying Relationship, whether or not earned by performance.
“Addendum”means an addendum to this Agreement executed in writing by both parties.
“Advance Rate”means a percentage of the face value of the Account Receivable (as may be adjusted by WHIZUNIK from time to time). The applicable Advance Rate will be disclosed to the Seller on the WHIZUNIK Platform or API (as applicable).
“Advance Rate Amount”means with respect to an Account Receivable the amount due with respect to such Account Receivable multiplied by the Advance Rate.
“Adverse Claim”means any Security Interest, netting, set-off, claim, defence or counterclaim, prohibition on assignment, negative pledge, including claims related to shipment, delivery, damage, defect, performance, failure to meet specifications, or failure to meet expressed or implied warranties.

“Applicable Law”means all existing and future laws, rules, regulations, statutes, treaties, codes, ordinances, Permits, certificates, orders and licenses of and interpretations by, any governmental authority.
